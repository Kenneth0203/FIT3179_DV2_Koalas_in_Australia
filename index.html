01 / Understanding the numbers

One species. Different regional estimates.

The monitoring programme reports separate estimates for the listed populations in Queensland, New South Wales and the ACT, and the unlisted populations in Victoria and South Australia. Each interval below shows the published lower and upper estimate.

Estimated koala populations, 2025

Hover over an interval for exact values. National bounds are reported separately and should not be calculated by adding the regional bounds. Source: National Koala Monitoring Program.

A larger estimate does not mean population growth. Survey coverage and modelling have improved. The 2023–2025 releases should not be treated as a comparable population trend.

03 / Queensland hospital records

What brings koalas into care?

Queensland’s KoalaBase extract contains 59,808 records from July 1996 to June 2025. These records describe sightings, threats and hospital interactions. They are not a population census, and changes may reflect reporting effort as well as real-world conditions.

KoalaBase records by year

Complete calendar years from 1997 to 2024 are shown. The area chart reflects records, not the number of koalas living in Queensland.

Where Queensland records concentrate.

The same hospital dataset supports three map idioms: proportional symbols for local government areas, a grid-density map of record locations, and a flow map connecting aggregated rescue and release locations.
